Order panels — parity statement (CPO 2026-08-06). Lite has exactly two order-panel implementations: contract (LiteContractOrderPanel) and spot (LiteOrderPanel, shared by daily stocks and quick rounds); their skeletons — amount input, presets, balance line, CTA chrome — are byte-identical. Contract/spot differences are deliberate and must not be "unified": title "Make your call" vs "Place your order", summary-row wording, CTA layout, risk vs execution footnote, Boost module + Est. auto-close (contract only), win-$ preset sublabels (spot only). Within spot, the quick-round page keeps its YOUR PICK question card with side selection outside the panel (desktop hideSideSelector) because rounds roll every few minutes with a fresh threshold and deadline, so the per-round question must stay visible at the top; daily stocks are one round per day and select in-panel. That is the ONLY structural difference allowed between the two spot pages. All side selection everywhere uses the single SideButton (density via size="compact") — no bespoke chips. "% say Up/Down" sublabels are banned as duplicates of the crowd bar.
